MEET OUR TEAM

At Way Logistics Ltd, our strength lies in the experience, discipline, and professionalism of our team. Each member plays a critical role in ensuring smooth operations, regulatory compliance, and dependable service delivery across all logistics activities.

Mahad Ahmed Hashim

Founder & Project Manager

Mahad Ahmed Hashim is the Founder of True Way Logistics Ltd and serves as the Project Manager, overseeing strategic direction, client engagement, and overall project coordination. With a strong understanding of customs procedures, logistics operations, and compliance requirements, he leads each engagement with a focus on accuracy, transparency, and efficiency.

As Project Manager, Mahad ensures that every shipment is planned, coordinated, and executed according to agreed timelines and regulatory standards. His hands-on approach and commitment to operational discipline form the foundation of True Way Logistics’ service philosophy.

Yahya Suleiman Mohammed

Operations Manager

Yahya Suleiman Mohammed leads day-to-day operational activities as the Operations Manager. He is responsible for coordinating port and airport operations, documentation flow, service partners, and shipment tracking from arrival to final delivery.

With a strong operational mindset, Yahya ensures that processes remain organized, compliant, and efficient. His role is central to maintaining service consistency, resolving operational challenges, and ensuring that every shipment moves smoothly through the logistics chain.

Amin Ali Hashim

Export Manager

Ibrahim Mohammed Abdullahi serves as the Export Manager, managing export documentation, regulatory requirements, and coordination with international partners. He ensures that export shipments comply with destination regulations and are processed accurately and on time.

His attention to detail and understanding of export compliance play a key role in supporting clients who rely on precise documentation and dependable export coordination.

💬A Message from the Managing Directo

At True Way Logistics Ltd, we believe that logistics is not just about moving cargo—it is about trust, responsibility, and accountability.

Every shipment we handle represents a commitment made by our clients to their customers and partners. We take that responsibility seriously. Our approach is grounded in compliance, careful planning, and clear communication, ensuring that each stage of the logistics process is managed professionally and transparently.

We understand the challenges businesses face when navigating customs procedures, documentation requirements, and operational uncertainties. That is why we focus on simplifying complex processes while maintaining strict adherence to regulatory standards. We do not believe in shortcuts; we believe in doing things the right way, every time.

Thank you for placing your trust in True Way Logistics Ltd. We look forward to supporting your operations and contributing to your success.
