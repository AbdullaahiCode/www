about us

True Way Logistics Ltd is a professional logistics, customs coordination, and supply chain services company dedicated to supporting businesses with reliable, compliant, and efficient cargo movement into Somalia, across all major Somali ports, and throughout East Africa. Founded with a strong commitment to integrity, accuracy, and operational excellence, we provide end‑to‑end logistics solutions covering customs clearance coordination, freight forwarding, port and airport handling, warehousing support, cargo consolidation, and last‑mile delivery. Our operations span key Somali gateways including Mogadishu Port, Kismayo Port, and Berbera Port, as well as Aden Adde International Airport. Beyond Somalia, True Way Logistics Ltd actively supports international trade lanes connecting China, Dubai (UAE), and Turkey, working through trusted partner agents and global logistics networks. As our business grows, we are committed to building long‑term partnerships with reputable logistics firms worldwide to ensure seamless, cost‑effective, and transparent cargo movement for our clients. We understand that every shipment represents a critical business commitment. That is why we operate with precision and accountability—ensuring documentation is accurate, regulatory procedures are properly followed, and timelines are respected. Our team coordinates closely with licensed customs agents, port authorities, shipping lines, airlines, and inland transport providers to minimize delays and safeguard our clients’ interests at every stage of the logistics process. At True Way Logistics Ltd, our mission is simple: to be a trusted logistics partner that connects Somalia and East Africa to global markets through professionalism, reliability, and compliance.
