OUR SERVICES

True Way Logistics Ltd provides comprehensive logistics, cargo, and supply chain solutions designed to support businesses operating in Somalia and across regional and international trade corridors. We deliver reliable, compliant, and well-coordinated services across sea, air, and inland logistics, supported by professionalism, transparency,
and operational excellence.

Our role is to act as a single logistics coordination partner, ensuring cargo moves efficiently from origin to final destination while meeting all customs and regulatory
requirements.



Customs Clearance Coordination

We provide professional customs clearance coordination for import and export cargo through Mogadishu Port, Kismayo Port, Berbera Port, and Aden Adde International
Airport. Our team ensures accurate documentation, regulatory compliance, and smooth cargo release to minimize delays and costs.
Services include:
Import and export customs documentation support
Duty and tax assessment coordination
Customs inspection and examination coordination
Regulatory compliance guidance
Cargo release facilitation

Sea Freight & Ocean Cargo (FCL & LCL)

We manage sea freight and ocean cargo shipments for Full Container Load (FCL) and Less-than-Container Load (LCL) cargo through trusted shipping lines and port
partners. Services include:
Containerized cargo coordination
Shipping line and bill of lading documentation support
Port operations coordination
Cargo discharge, handling, and release support
Breakbulk and general cargo coordination

Air Freight & Air Cargo Services

For time-sensitive and high-value shipments, we provide efficient air freight and air cargo coordination services through Aden Adde International Airport, ensuring fast clearance and timely cargo availability.
Services include:
Air waybill (AWB) processing support
Air cargo documentation coordination
Priority and express cargo handling
Customs clearance coordination
Expedited cargo release support

Inland Transportation & Domestic Logistics

We provide reliable inland transportation and domestic logistics coordination, connecting ports and airports to warehouses, project sites, distribution centers, and final destinations across Somalia.
Services include:
Port and airport cargo pickup coordination
Local trucking and inland transport coordination
Door-to-door delivery services
Handling supervision and delivery reporting

Warehousing, Storage & Distribution Support

We offer secure and well-coordinated warehousing and storage solutions to support cargo holding, consolidation, inventory management, and distribution. Services include:
Short-term and long-term storage solutions
Cargo consolidation and deconsolidation
Inventory coordination and monitoring
Dispatch and last-mile delivery support

Cargo Handling & Cargo Coordination

We provide professional cargo handling supervision and cargo coordination services to ensure cargo is safely managed at every stage of the logistics process. Services include:
Cargo receiving and release coordination
Container cargo handling supervision
Loose, consolidated (LCL), and general cargo coordination
Cargo inspection and verification support
Cargo delivery coordination

Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Services

Comprehensive <>3PL solutions, including shipment coordination, inventory support, transport management, and end-to-end supply chain oversight.

Project Cargo & Specialized Shipments

We handle project cargo and specialized shipments requiring detailed planning, technical coordination, and controlled execution. Services include:
Project cargo planning and logistics coordination
Heavy-lift and oversized cargo handling support
Specialized documentation coordination
Route planning and execution supervision

Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Services

We provide Third-Party Logistics (3PL) services, acting as a single point of contact to manage multiple logistics activities and service providers across the supply chain. Services include:
End-to-end logistics coordination
Supply chain and transport management support
Inventory and distribution coordination
Multi-vendor and partner coordination

International Cargo & Freight Coordination

We coordinate international cargo movements through established global trade lanes, working with trusted overseas logistics partners. Trade lanes include:
China → Somalia
UAE (Dubai) → Somalia
Turkey → Somalia
Regional East Africa cargo movements
Services include:
Pre-shipment planning and documentation review
Cargo tracking and shipment visibility
Coordination with origin and destination partners
Delivery and handover supervision

Logistics Consultation & Advisory Services

We support first-time and experienced importers with professional logistics consultation and advisory services.
Services include:
Import and export process guidance
Documentation review and compliance advisory
Customs clearance planning support
Operational logistics consultation
