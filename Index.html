about us

True Way Logistics Ltd is a professional logistics, customs coordination, and supply chain services company dedicated to supporting businesses with reliable, compliant, and efficient cargo movement into Somalia, across all major Somali ports, and throughout East Africa. Founded with a strong commitment to integrity, accuracy, and operational excellence, we provide end‑to‑end logistics solutions covering customs clearance coordination, freight forwarding, port and airport handling, warehousing support, cargo consolidation, and last‑mile delivery. Our operations span key Somali gateways including Mogadishu Port, Kismayo Port, and Berbera Port, as well as Aden Adde International Airport. Beyond Somalia, True Way Logistics Ltd actively supports international trade lanes connecting China, Dubai (UAE), and Turkey, working through trusted partner agents and global logistics networks. As our business grows, we are committed to building long‑term partnerships with reputable logistics firms worldwide to ensure seamless, cost‑effective, and transparent cargo movement for our clients. We understand that every shipment represents a critical business commitment. That is why we operate with precision and accountability—ensuring documentation is accurate, regulatory procedures are properly followed, and timelines are respected. Our team coordinates closely with licensed customs agents, port authorities, shipping lines, airlines, and inland transport providers to minimize delays and safeguard our clients’ interests at every stage of the logistics process. At True Way Logistics Ltd, our mission is simple: to be a trusted logistics partner that connects Somalia and East Africa to global markets through professionalism, reliability, and compliance.

OUR SERVICES

True Way Logistics Ltd provides reliable logistics and supply chain solutions across sea, air, and inland operations in Somalia. We support businesses with compliant, efficient, and well-coordinated cargo handling services.

Customs Clearance Coordination

Accurate and compliant customs clearance coordination at Mogadishu Port, Kismayo Port, Berbera Port, and Aden Adde International Airport, ensuring all documentation and regulatory requirements are properly managed.

Sea Freight (FCL & LCL)

Reliable handling and coordination of full container load (FCL) and less than container load (LCL) shipments, including port operations, documentation, and cargo release support.

Air Freight

Efficient air cargo coordination for time-sensitive and high-value shipments, with end-to-end handling from origin to final destination.

Inland Transportation

Safe, timely, and well-managed cargo movement from ports or airports to warehouses, project sites, or final delivery points.

Warehousing & Storage

Secure warehousing and cargo storage solutions to support distribution, inventory management, and last-mile delivery.

Project Cargo & Specialized Shipments

Professional planning and execution of oversized, heavy-lift, and non-standard cargo, including route planning and coordination with relevant authorities.

Third-Party Logistics (3PL) Services

Comprehensive <>3PL solutions, including shipment coordination, inventory support, transport management, and end-to-end supply chain oversight.

Cargo & Logistics Coordination

Acting as a dedicated logistics coordinator, we manage multiple service providers, timelines, and documentation to ensure seamless cargo movement across international and domestic trade lanes.

International Trade Lane Coordination

Cargo coordination and freight support for shipments originating from China, UAE (Dubai), and Turkey, working through trusted global logistics partners.

At True Way Logistics Ltd, we don’t just move cargo—we deliver reliability, compliance, visibility, and peace of mind for every shipment.
